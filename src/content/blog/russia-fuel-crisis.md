Russia is facing a widening fuel supply crisis affecting regional distribution hubs and domestic reserves. 

### Key Strategic Indicators

* **Logistical Bottlenecks:** Refinery downtime combined with targeted transportation disruptions has restricted supply lines across multiple administrative regions.
* **Domestic Restrictions:** Authorities have implemented purchase ceilings and prioritized military logistical corridors over consumer retail access.
* **Import Shifts:** Unprecedented fuel flows and trade renegotiations highlight the urgent pressure on the domestic refining matrix.

The strategic implications extend directly into operational logistics, impacting both regional mobility and energy export revenues.
