Russia has historically stood as a primary supplier of crude feedstocks across Eurasian trade routes. Recent bilateral fuel procurement data, however, reflects a significant structural reversal.

### Key Observations

* **Refining Constraints:** Outages across key processing complexes have forced state entities to secure refined volumes abroad.
* **Logistical Complexity:** Transport corridors via maritime and pipeline routes are operating under elevated transit costs and insurance premiums.
* **Market Exposure:** The dynamic exposes domestic regional retail networks to supply shocks despite vast upstream production capacities.
