Securing distributed energy nodes—ranging from transformer substations to regional pumping facilities—presents a fundamental resource-allocation challenge for modern air defense systems.

### Tactical Dilemmas

* **Cost Asymmetry:** Utilizing multi-million-dollar interceptor missiles against massed, low-cost loitering munitions imposes severe budgetary and inventory attrition.
* **Mobile Interceptor Teams:** Expanding reliance on mobile ground units equipped with searchlights, electro-optical trackers, and autocannons to preserve surface-to-air missile stockpiles.
* **Passive Grid Hardening:** Concrete blast walls, mesh netting, and redundant autotransformer inventories remain the most cost-effective mitigation layers against fragmentation damage.

Sustaining electrical continuity into the winter requires integrating physical dispersion strategies with layered, multi-tier air defense perimeters.
