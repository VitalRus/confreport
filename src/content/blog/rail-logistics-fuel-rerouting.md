As refining hubs face maintenance intervals and logistical disruptions, domestic distribution networks increasingly rely on long-haul rail networks to balance regional fuel deficits.

### Critical Friction Points

* **Rolling Stock Allocation:** Tanker cars (*tsisterny*) are experiencing extended turnaround times due to rerouted supply lines and congested switching yards.
* **Priority Freight Scheduling:** Passenger and critical defense logistics routinely preempt industrial bulk cargo, creating localized delivery delays for retail depots.
* **Substation Grid Dependencies:** Electric locomotive reliability is vulnerable to recurring regional power fluctuations, forcing diesel engine redeployments.

These structural dynamics reflect how supply disruptions propagate beyond refining complexes into systemic ground logistics challenges.
