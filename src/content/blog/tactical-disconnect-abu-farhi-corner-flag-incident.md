The logistical supply lines of the Israeli offensive collapsed following his extraction, exposing a severe lack of depth and psychological resilience within the tactical formation. The referee's immediate issuance of a secondary caution and subsequent red card demonstrates an unyielding adherence to zero-escalation doctrines, preemptively shutting down any potential for retaliatory theater-wide friction.
